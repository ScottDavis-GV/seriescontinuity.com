Getting your licence key…

This page shows your licence key after you buy, renew or upgrade Series Continuity. The link that brought you here has no order in it.

If you have just paid, write to hello@techniquestassociates.com with your receipt and we’ll send your key.

Your payment went through, and we need to look at your order before your licence is ready. There is nothing you need to do: we’ll be in touch by email, at the address you gave at checkout.

If you have a question in the meantime, write to hello@techniquestassociates.com with your receipt.

Adding the key to Series Continuity

  1. Open Series Continuity.
  2. Go to General Settings → License.
  3. Paste the key into Add a license key and choose Add key.

A key works on up to three of your computers. To move it to another, remove it from the old one first, in the same place.

Save this key now, for example in a password manager. It is shown on this page for 24 hours and is not emailed. If you lose it, write to hello@techniquestassociates.com with your licence ID or your receipt and we’ll send it again.

Getting your updated key

For your security, a renewed or upgraded key is not shown here. Your copy of Series Continuity collects it, using the key it already has:

  1. Open Series Continuity on a computer where your licence is already set up.
  2. Go to General Settings → License.
  3. Choose Check for updated key.

Do this on each of your computers. It doesn’t use up another computer. If your licence isn’t set up anywhere just now, write to hello@techniquestassociates.com with your licence ID and receipt.
