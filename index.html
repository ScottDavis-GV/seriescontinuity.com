Keep every book consistent with the last.

Series Continuity is a desktop app that reads your chapters, builds a series bible from them, and checks each new chapter against it. The colour of her eyes, the name of the street, the day it happened: the same in book four as in book one.

What it does

A series bible that keeps itself

Builds the bible from your chapters

Characters, places, the timeline, objects and the facts of your world are drawn out of the chapters you have written and kept in one place, each with the chapter it came from.

Checks new chapters against it

Before a contradiction reaches a reader, Series Continuity shows you where a new chapter disagrees with what the series has already established.

Keeps your work safe

Your series bible stays on your own computer, with dated backups, a history of every change, and a way back to any of them.

Series Continuity is in development. It will be offered in Basic and Advanced editions, with a free trial.
